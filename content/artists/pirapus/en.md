Pirapus is a drum and bass duo from Christchurch, New Zealand. George Murray plays keys, and Ben "Tex" Hollamby, who comes from Houston, plays guitar; they were in the same first-year hall at the University of Canterbury, and the duo grew out of a jam in Tex's room after a party. Pirapus had been Tex's name as a hip-hop beatmaker. The pair describe their music as "future drum & bass", have collaborated with Andromedik, SOTA and REAPER, and have played Tomorrowland, Rampage and Beats For Love.

Their kit, EVERYNITE, is one of the NIGHTMODE II Music Kits, which Valve released with NIGHTMODE Records in May 2026.
