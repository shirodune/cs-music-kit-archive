Ben Bromfield is a composer, songwriter and jazz pianist who works mostly in television. He got his start scoring animated series for DreamWorks, among them The Boss Baby: Back in Business, with Ryan Elder. In 2023 he and Lili Haydn were nominated for an Emmy for Outstanding Original Music and Lyrics, for "Marriage Is a Dungeon" from Ginny & Georgia.

His first kit, M.U.D.D. FORCE (Masterminds, 2020), he wrote with Tree Adams, his mentor. His own kit, Rabbit Hole, followed in Masterminds 2 in 2024.
