ISOxo is an American DJ and producer based in San Diego; his background is half Indonesian, half Filipino. When 88rising took on its first two dance music acts, they were ISOxo and Knock2, and the label put out his debut album, kidsgonemad!, on 20 October 2023. The two later teamed up as ISOKNOCK for 4EVR, an album co-produced by RL Grime that 88rising released on 1 August 2024.

His kit, inhuman, came out in February 2024 in the NIGHTMODE Music Kit Box.
