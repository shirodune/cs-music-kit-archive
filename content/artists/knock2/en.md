Richard Singphiphat Nakhonethap, a Laotian-American from San Diego, met ISOxo at 14, when both were booked to DJ at the San Diego County Fair. As Knock2 he makes bass house mixed with trap; with ISOxo he also performs as ISOKNOCK, whose album 4EVR came out on 88rising in 2024, the year they played Coachella. His own releases include the EPs 2HEARTs (2021) and ROOM202 (2023) and his first solo album, nolimit, released by 88rising in January 2025.

He has two kits: dashstar*, in the Initiators Music Kit Box of August 2022, and Make U SWEAT!, in the NIGHTMODE Music Kit Box of February 2024.
