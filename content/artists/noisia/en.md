Noisia are Nik Roos, Martijn van Sonderen and Thijs de Vlieger, an electronic trio from Groningen in the Netherlands. They broke through in 2003, with music that spans drum and bass, dubstep, breakbeat and house. Their albums include Split the Atom (2010) and Outer Edges (2016), and they scored the game DmC: Devil May Cry.

Sharpened was one of the nine kits that music kits launched with in October 2014. In 2019 Noisia announced they would split; they played their last show in August 2022. In September 2026 the three announced their return, with a first show at Melkweg in Amsterdam on 4 December.
