3kliksphilip is a YouTuber who posts videos about Counter-Strike; by August 2022 the channel had passed one million subscribers. He also makes music. His Bandcamp page holds the albums In The Face, Audio Crime and Grandparent Edition, a release titled 3kliksphilip 2008 - 2012, and Audio Crime 2, released in December 2023.

His kit, Heading for the Source, came in the Initiators Music Kit Box in August 2022.
