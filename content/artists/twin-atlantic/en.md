Twin Atlantic formed in Glasgow, Scotland, in 2006; its current members are Sam McTrusty, who sings and plays guitar, and bassist Ross McNae. Great Divide (2014), their third album, peaked at No. 6 in the UK. Its single Heart and Soul reached No. 17 and won Best Independent Track at the 2014 Association of Independent Music Awards. At the 2016 SSE Scottish Music Awards the band was named Best UK Band.

Their kit, GLA, came in the StatTrak™ Radicals Box in November 2016.
