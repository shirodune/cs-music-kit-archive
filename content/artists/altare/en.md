Altare is a producer from Singapore who calls himself a "Wave/Trap producer". He has been releasing music since 2019, on labels including Sable Valley, MONTA Records and Ophelia Records, and has worked with REMNANT.exe, Bafu and Dravyn.

His kit, Change My Mind, is one of the NIGHTMODE II Music Kits, which Valve released with NIGHTMODE Records in May 2026.
