New Beat Fund are a Los Angeles band who describe their music as "g-punk". Their first EP was a do-it-yourself release, recorded at home. In 2014 Music Connection reported that they had signed with Red Bull Records after meeting its A&R man Kenny "Tick" Salcido near Hollywood, and that the meeting also led to a run of shows opening for Blink-182; at that time the band was a four-piece and was recording its first full-length album for the label. Their album Sponge Fingerz came out on Red Bull Records in June 2015.

Their kit, Sponge Fingerz, came out in September 2015.
