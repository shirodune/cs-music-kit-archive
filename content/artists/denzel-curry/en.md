Denzel Curry is a rapper from Carol City in Miami Gardens, Florida, born in 1995. As a teenager he belonged to SpaceGhostPurrp's collective Raider Klan, and he was still in high school when his first album, Nostalgic 64, came out in 2013. XXL named him to its 2016 Freshman Class. Ta13oo (2018), Zuu (2019) and Melt My Eyez See Your Future (2022) all reached the Billboard 200, the last climbing to No. 17 after an extended edition was released.

His kit, ULTIMATE, was released on its own in February 2023, outside any music kit box.
