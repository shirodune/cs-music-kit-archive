Repiet is Pieter Beeldman, an Amsterdam DJ and producer who makes future house and has released on labels such as STMPD RCRDS, Revealed Recordings and Virgin. He has been producing since he was nine, though his first interest was visual art. Julia Kleijn, also from Amsterdam, is a singer and songwriter who started out as a child actress and singer in music theatre. The two had worked together before, on Feels Like (La La La) and on Echo, a 2023 single with RSCL on Gemstone Records.

Their kit, On And On, is one of the NIGHTMODE II Music Kits, which Valve released with NIGHTMODE Records in May 2026.
