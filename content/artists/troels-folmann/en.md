Troels Brun Folmann is a Danish composer, born in Copenhagen. As in-house composer at Crystal Dynamics he scored Tomb Raider: Legend (2006), which won the BAFTA for Original Score in October 2006, and Tomb Raider: Anniversary (2007), and he wrote the main theme of Tomb Raider: Underworld (2008). He has also written music for film trailers, among them Spider-Man 3 and 10,000 BC. When Tonehammer, the sample company he co-owned, split in two in 2011, he went on to lead one of the new companies, 8Dio, with Tawnia Knox; it makes virtual instruments for composers.

His kit, Uber Blasto Phone, came out in September 2015.
