Adam Beyer is a Swedish DJ and producer, born in Stockholm in 1976. He started out in techno and founded Drumcode, a label that dates from 1996; his releases include the album Decoded (1996) and the single Teach Me (2014). He first entered DJ Magazine's Top 100 DJs poll in 2003 and placed 48th in both 2019 and 2020.

His kit, Red Room, came out in August 2025 in the Deluge Music Kit Box.
