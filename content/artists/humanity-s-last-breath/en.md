Humanity's Last Breath come from Helsingborg in Sweden. Buster Odeholm and Kristoffer Nilsson started the band in 2009, and Odeholm, who has also drummed for Vildhjarta and Thrown, remains its main songwriter and producer; Filip Danielsson has been the singer since 2016. The band's polyrhythmic mix of deathcore, djent and doom has a name of its own: they call it "thall". Their self-titled first album came out in 2013, followed by Abyssal in 2019, then Välde in 2021 and Ashen in 2023, both on Unique Leader Records.

Their kit, Void, was part of the Initiators Music Kit Box in August 2022.
