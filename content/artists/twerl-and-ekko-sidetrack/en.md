TWERL and Ekko & Sidetrack are producers from Perth, Australia, who have been friends for more than ten years. TWERL is Jayden Healey, whose productions range from trap to bass house; his credits include work with ShockOne and an official remix for Diplo. Ekko & Sidetrack are the brothers Jeff and Jonathan Hansen, a drum and bass duo. They signed with Viper Recordings in 2018 and played their first European shows that year, at Let It Roll and Liquicity Festival; in 2019 their track with ShockOne, Klaxon, appeared on his album A Dark Machine.

Their kit, Under Bright Lights, came out in February 2024 in the NIGHTMODE Music Kit Box.
