Sullivan King is the Los Angeles musician Keaton Prescott, born in 1994, who brings electric guitar and heavy metal into dubstep DJ sets. He has played live since 2014 and toured the US several times. His studio albums have come out on three different labels: Show Some Teeth (Kannibalen Records, 2019), Loud (Hopeless Records, 2021) and Thrones of Blood (Monstercat, 2023). Among his collaborations are "Domination" with Kayzo and Papa Roach and several tracks with Excision.

His kit, Lock Me Up, was part of the Initiators Music Kit Box in August 2022.
