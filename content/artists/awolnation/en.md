AWOLNATION's song "Sail" peaked at No. 17 on the Billboard Hot 100, and in June 2021 the RIAA certified it diamond. It came from Megalithic Symphony, the first album by the Los Angeles rock band, which Red Bull Records released in March 2011. The band's founder and singer, Aaron Bruno, previously of Under the Influence of Giants and Home Town Hero, had signed with that label after it let him record in its Los Angeles studio in 2009. The second album, Run, followed in March 2015.

Their kit, I Am, came out in September 2015.
