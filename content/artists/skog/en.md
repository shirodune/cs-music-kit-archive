Jocke Skog, who makes his kits as Skog, is a Swedish producer, engineer and musician. He plays keyboards and sings backing vocals in Clawfinger, a rap metal band formed in Stockholm in 1989; he and Zak Tell had met at work, at Rosenlund Hospital. He began on piano in the late 1970s. Since the mid-1990s he has also produced and mixed records for other bands, and in 2001 Clawfinger and the members of Meshuggah bought the Fear and Loathing studio together. He has made preset packs for Toontrack.

He made three kits: Metal, one of the nine kits that music kits launched with in October 2014; II-Headshot, in September 2015; and III-Arena, in the StatTrak™ Radicals Box in November 2016.
