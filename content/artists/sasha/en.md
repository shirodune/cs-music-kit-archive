In 2000 a DJ Magazine poll named Sasha the world's No. 1 DJ. Born Alexander Coe in Bangor, Wales, in 1969, he made mix albums with John Digweed such as Northern Exposure (1996), and his own albums include Airdrawndagger (2002) and Scene Delete (2016). For the PlayStation he scored the game Wipeout 3, and a Grammy nomination came for his remix of Felix da Housecat's "Watching Cars Go By".

His kit, LNOE, was one of the nine kits that music kits launched with in October 2014.
