Perfect World is a Beijing games and film company, founded in 2004, that distributes Counter-Strike 2 and Dota 2 in China. Its kits come from the team that runs the Chinese version of Counter-Strike, which brings in outside musicians for each one and presents both under its intangible cultural heritage programme, started in 2022.

花脸 Hua Lian (Painted Face), a Qinqiang opera and rock song written and sung by Chinese opera and music professionals, came out in September 2022. Ay Hey, Zhuang folk song mixed with electronic music and created with Guangxi Arts University, followed in November 2024. Neither came in a music kit box.
