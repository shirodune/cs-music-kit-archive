Matt Levine spent more than ten years from 2005 in the music department of Sony Computer Entertainment America, working on 37 of its titles; among them were The Last of Us, Uncharted 3 and Infamous: First Light, for which he wrote music and remixes. He set up his production company, Uncle Vector's Audio Lab, in 1998, and has taught composition and production for games and film at the San Francisco Conservatory of Music since 2015. He studied at the California Institute of the Arts (BFA, 1992).

His kits are Drifter, in the Masterminds Music Kit Box of April 2020, and Agency, in the Masterminds 2 Music Kit Box of August 2024.
