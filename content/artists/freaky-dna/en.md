Freaky DNA is the name under which the composer, sound designer and teacher Leonard J. Paul performs; when Valve announced the Tacticians box, it listed him as "Leonard Paul (Freaky DNA)". Paul holds an honours degree in computer science from Simon Fraser University in British Columbia, with a minor in music focused on electroacoustics. By 2015 he had spent twenty years composing, designing sound and writing code for games, among them NBA Jam 2010 and NHL 11. As Freaky DNA he wrote chiptune music for Retro City Rampage, next to virt and Norrin Radd, and has played live electronic sets in cities such as Osaka, Berlin and Amsterdam. He also composed the music of the documentary The Corporation and co-founded the School of Video Game Audio.

His kit, Vici, is in the Tacticians Music Kit Box of August 2021.
