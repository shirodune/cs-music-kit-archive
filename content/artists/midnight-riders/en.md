Left 4 Dead 2, Valve's 2009 game, invents a hard rock band, the Midnight Riders. Their stage turns up in the Dark Carnival campaign: the band has left it behind, and the survivors switch on its light show to signal a helicopter pilot who can rescue them. Mike Morasky, a composer at Valve, wrote the band's music and played the bass, guitar and keyboard parts himself. An in-character website for the band carries a tour blog.

Their kit, All I Want for Christmas, came in December 2014 as a holiday-themed offer for a limited time.
