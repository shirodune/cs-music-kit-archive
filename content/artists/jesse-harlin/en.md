Jesse Harlin writes music for games. He spent ten years as staff composer and music supervisor at LucasArts, and for the Special Edition of The Secret of Monkey Island he redid all of the music himself. In 2013 he set up his own company, Dunderpate Music. His credits include Star Wars: Republic Commando, The Force Unleashed, Star Wars: The Old Republic, Mafia III, CounterSpy, Yoku's Island Express and Valorant. For six years Game Developer Magazine ran his column, "Aural Fixation", and for Star Wars he created a language for the ancestors of Boba Fett.

His kit, Astro Bellum, is in the Tacticians Music Kit Box of August 2021.
