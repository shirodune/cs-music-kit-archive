Michael Bross wrote music for Oddworld Inhabitants' games, including Oddworld: Munch's Oddysee and Oddworld: Stranger's Wrath. In 2011 Insomniac Games announced him as the composer of Ratchet & Clank: All 4 One, with more than four hours of music planned; the Game Audio Network Guild now calls him the series' "staple composer". Across more than twenty years in game audio he has worked as a composer, sound designer and audio director on over 200 titles, for companies such as Valve, Sony, EA and Wargaming.

His kit, Invasion!, came out in September 2015.
