Darren Korb is the audio director and composer at Supergiant Games, an independent studio whose co-founder Amir Rao is a childhood friend of his. Born in San Jose, California, in 1983, he wrote the music for the studio's Bastion (2011), Transistor (2014), Pyre (2017), Hades (2020) and Hades II (2025). Bastion won him Best Original Score at the 2011 Spike Video Game Awards, along with Best Song in a Game for "Build That Wall"; his Hades music was nominated at The Game Awards and the BAFTA Games Awards. He also acts: in Hades he is the voice of Zagreus, the main character.

His kit, the Hades Music Kit, came to Counter-Strike: Global Offensive in March 2021.
