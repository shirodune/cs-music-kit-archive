Matt Lange is a DJ and producer from Manhattan who lives in Los Angeles. His debut album, Ephemera, came out on deadmau5's label Mau5trap in September 2015 and was a techno record, a change from the house releases he had put out before on labels such as Armada Music and Anjunadeep. With Glenn Morrison he co-produced "Goodbye", nominated for Dance Recording of the Year at the 2015 Juno Awards. Thirty Seconds to Mars' album AMERICA credits him as a producer and co-writer, and he has done sound design for entries in the Fast & Furious, Deadpool and Transformers franchises.

His kit, IsoRhythm, came out in February 2015.
