Born in Rio de Janeiro in 1972, Amon Tobin left Brazil with his family at the age of two and lived in several countries before settling in Brighton, England, as a teenager; in 2002 he moved to Montreal. The electronic musician and producer signed with Ninja Tune in 1996, and the label put out albums of his such as Bricolage (1997), Supermodified (2000), Foley Room (2007) and ISAM (2011). In 2019 he started releasing on a new label, Nomark Records. Games he has scored include Tom Clancy's Splinter Cell: Chaos Theory (2005) and inFamous (2009). He also records as Two Fingers, Figueroa and Only Child Tyrant.

His kit, All for Dust, went on sale in Counter-Strike: Global Offensive in June 2020.
