Juelz is the Vancouver producer and DJ Julian Ching, who makes bass and trap music. Violin came first: he started lessons at five and was classically trained at the Royal Conservatory of Music. He came to trap through producers such as Baauer and Cashmere Cat, whom he still counted among his influences in 2017. His first album, Floorspace, came out in June 2023 on Sable Valley, the label run by RL Grime.

Shooters, his first kit, was in the Initiators Music Kit Box in August 2022. Floorspace followed in the Deluge Music Kit Box in August 2025.
