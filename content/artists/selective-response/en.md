Selective Response is a techno DJ and producer from Orange County in Southern California. He came to techno from hardcore bands and from trance, drum and bass and psytrance, which he made under an earlier alias. The Selective Response project officially began in 2018, and a year later he founded his own label, Crisis Of Man. He has described his sound as "extremely aggressive and hard-hitting industrial" techno.

His kit, No Love Only Pleasure, came in the Deluge Music Kit Box in August 2025.
