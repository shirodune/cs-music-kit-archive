Ian Hultquist is an American composer, born in 1985, who scores films and television series. His work includes Andrew Rossi's documentary Ivory Tower (2014), David Dastmalchian's film Animals, the Apple TV+ series Dickinson, written together with Drum & Lace, and AMC's The Walking Dead: Dead City. A Berklee College of Music graduate, he co-founded the band Passion Pit in 2007 and was its music director on tour until he left in October 2014. DOC NYC named him to its 40 Under 40 list in 2020, mentioning his work on the Netflix series How to Fix a Drug Scandal.

His kit, Lion's Mouth, came out in September 2015.
