HEALTH is an industrial rock band from Los Angeles: Jake Duzsik on vocals and rhythm guitar, John Famiglietti on bass and electronics, and B.J. Miller on drums. They grew out of the city's underground experimental scene, and their first album, Health (2007), was recorded at The Smell, a noise and experimental venue there. In 2012 they wrote the score for Max Payne 3, a Rockstar Games release. Their collaborations include Isn't Everyone with Nine Inch Nails (2021) and Mean with Chelsea Wolfe (2025).

Their kit, RAT WARS, came out in August 2025 in the Deluge Music Kit Box.
