KILL SCRIPT is a techno DJ and producer whose work draws on science fiction, with 3D animation as part of his visuals. His first single, STASIS, appeared on NIGHTMODE's Cyberpunk compilation, and the label went on to release his debut EP FIRST CONTAKT in 2021 and then VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENT. At EDC Las Vegas in May 2023 he played a sunset set of almost 90 minutes on the circuitGROUNDS stage; two unreleased tracks he played during that set became the EXTRACTION EP that October. Techno, he has said, is "the root of KILL SCRIPT", but the project's progressive side matters to him as much.

His kit, All Night, came out in February 2024 in the NIGHTMODE Music Kit Box.
