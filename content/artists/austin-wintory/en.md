Austin Wintory is an American composer for games and film, born in Denver in 1984. He is best known for the music of Journey: in December 2012 it became the first game score ever nominated for a Grammy, in the category Best Score Soundtrack for Visual Media. His other game scores include The Banner Saga trilogy, ABZÛ and Assassin's Creed Syndicate.

In Counter-Strike he was there from the start: Desert Fire was one of the nine kits that music kits launched with in October 2014. Bachram, with the vocals of Peter Hollens, followed in the Masterminds box, Mocha Petal in Tacticians and The Devil Went Clubbing in Georgia in Masterminds 2.
