Mateo Messina is an American composer, born in Seattle in 1972. He composed the score for Jason Reitman's Juno (2007); the film's soundtrack album won the Grammy for Best Compilation Soundtrack Album for Motion Picture, Television or Other Visual Media. Butter, The Angriest Man in Brooklyn and Barely Lethal are among his other films, and his television work includes the series Superstore and Casual. Messina also writes symphonies, more than twenty so far. He and his team turn each premiere into a benefit concert for families at Seattle Children's Hospital, and these concerts have raised over $2,000,000.

His kit, For No Mankind, came out in February 2015.
