Proxy is the stage name of Yevgeny Pozharnov, a Russian electronic producer and DJ. He came up through Tiga's label Turbo Recordings and also ran his own label, MAKO Records. His early tracks include "Raven" and "Who Are You?", and he has remixed Peaches, Tiga, Boys Noize and Moby. His debut album came in two parts: Music from the Eastblock Jungles, Part I in late 2012 and Part II in early 2013.

His kit, Battlepack, came out in September 2015.
