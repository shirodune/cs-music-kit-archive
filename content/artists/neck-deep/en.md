Neck Deep is a pop-punk band from Wrexham, Wales, formed in 2012 after singer Ben Barlow met guitarist Lloyd Roberts. Their album Life's Not Out to Get You (2015) reached No. 8 in the UK and No. 17 on the Billboard 200, and The Peace and the Panic (2017) entered the Billboard 200 at No. 4. In 2014 they were named Best British Newcomer at the Kerrang! Awards.

Their first kit shares its name with the 2015 album and came in the StatTrak™ Radicals Box in November 2016. The Lowlife Pack followed in March 2021, with songs from All Distortions Are Intentional (2020), which reached No. 4 in the UK.
