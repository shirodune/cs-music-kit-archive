Alexander Gumuchian, born in Vancouver in 1995, is a Canadian rapper, singer and songwriter who records as bbno$, short for "baby no money". He turned to music in 2014, making it with a group of friends, after a back injury ruled out a career as a swimmer. From September 2016 he uploaded songs to SoundCloud under the name bbnomula, where they soon drew millions of streams. "Lalala", his 2019 single with the producer Y2K, peaked at No. 10 in Canada and No. 55 on the Billboard Hot 100, and the RIAA certified it triple platinum in 2023. He won the Juno Fan Choice Award in 2025 and again in 2026.

His kit, u mad!, came out in October 2021.
