Chipzel is the stage name of Niamh Houston, a chiptune musician and game composer from Strabane, Northern Ireland, born in 1991, who plays the Game Boy. Terry Cavanagh put her track "Courtesy" in Hexagon, a game he made for a game jam, and then asked her for tracks for its extended version, Super Hexagon (2012); in 2019 she made both the music and the sound effects of his Dicey Dungeons. At Gateway Interactive's suggestion, her 2013 album Spectra became a racing game of the same name, released in 2015, the year her soundtrack for Interstellaria also came out. Her official site calls her "BAFTA nominated".

Her kit, ~Yellow Magic~, is in the Tacticians Music Kit Box of August 2021.
