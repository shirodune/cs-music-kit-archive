Kelly Bailey grew up in New Zealand and was a product unit manager at Microsoft before he went to Valve, where, in Forbes' words, he was "pretty much the 5th employee Valve ever hired". He created all of the music and sound effects for Half-Life (1998) and wrote the music for Half-Life 2. He was also one of four Valve employees whose faces were used for Gordon Freeman in Half-Life 2's promotional materials. At the time of Half-Life he sang lead in the Seattle band Lucy's Fishing Trip.

His kit, Hazardous Environments, came out in September 2015.
