Born in Eura, Finland, in 1975, Toni-Ville Henrik Virtanen works as a DJ and producer under the name Darude, mostly in trance and house. Sandstorm, an instrumental released in late 1999, led his debut album Before the Storm (2000). In the UK it climbed to No. 3, a position no record by a Finnish artist had reached there before. Before the Storm brought him three Finnish Grammy awards, with worldwide sales of 800,000 copies. Finland sent him to the 2019 Eurovision Song Contest, where he and singer Sebastian Rejman performed Look Away; they came last in the first semi-final.

His kit, Moments CSGO, came out in September 2015.
