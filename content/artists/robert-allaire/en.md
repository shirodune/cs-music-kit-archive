Robert Allaire writes music for film, television and games from Los Angeles. His film scores include Some Kind of Hate and Too Late; on television he worked on Stargate: Origins and Better Call Saul Presents: Slippin' Jimmy; in games he scored Sony's Kill Strain and wrote several League of Legends themes for Riot Games. He also writes string arrangements for singers such as Gin Wigmore, Clinton Kane and David Kushner. He holds an MFA in composition from the California Institute of the Arts.

His kit, Insurgency, was one of the nine kits that music kits launched with in October 2014.
