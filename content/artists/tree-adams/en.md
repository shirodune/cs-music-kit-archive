Tree Adams is a composer for film, television and games, working from his studio in Los Angeles. In more than 25 years his credits have come to include the series Californication, NCIS: New Orleans, Lethal Weapon and The 100 and the films Walking Tall and Dawn of the Dead; his main title theme for Canterbury's Law was nominated for a Primetime Emmy in 2008. He also spent a decade on the road as the singer, guitarist and main songwriter of the band The Hatters.

His first kit, M.U.D.D. FORCE (Masterminds, 2020), he wrote with Ben Bromfield, who calls him his mentor. Seventh Moon followed in Masterminds 2 in 2024.
