Starjunk 95 is a music project based in Tokyo. Its Bandcamp page names no members. Instead it frames the project as a fictional broadcaster from space, "the grooviest radio station of Starjunk City", playing new music with a retro flavour.

Its kit, Industrial Sunset Memories, came out in September 2026 as a single kit rather than in a box.
