ALRT is Irad Brant, a DJ and producer born in Israel and based in Los Angeles, who started the project in 2018. He sums up his approach as "NRG": high-energy music regardless of genre, moving between house, hardstyle, dubstep and trap, with much of its inspiration in the rave culture of the 1990s and acts such as The Prodigy and Daft Punk. His records have come out on Ultra, Musical Freedom, STMPD RCRDS and Dim Mak, among others, and he started his own label, NRG Records.

His kit, DOPAMINE HIT, is one of the NIGHTMODE II Music Kits, which Valve released with NIGHTMODE Records in May 2026.
