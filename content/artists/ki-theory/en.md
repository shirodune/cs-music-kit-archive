Joel Burleson, from Richmond, Virginia, records and produces as Ki:Theory, pronounced "key theory". His music mixes rock and electronics, on albums such as KITTY HAWK (2013), Silence (2017) and HMNS (2025). Much of it has been used on screen: the title track of KITTY HAWK in the trailer for FIFA 14, his cover of "Stand by Me" in the TV series The Following and its 2014 Super Bowl spot, and his tracks and custom scores in trailers for Netflix productions such as Money Heist.

His kit, MOLOTOV, came out in September 2015.
