Half-Life: Alyx is a virtual reality first-person shooter that Valve developed and published; it came out for Windows on 23 March 2020. The score is Mike Morasky's, whose other work for Valve includes Portal 2 and Team Fortress 2; he has named industrial music, Nine Inch Nails among it, as an inspiration. After release the soundtrack appeared bit by bit, one part for each of the game's eleven chapters; the Steam edition holds 72 tracks, which Valve describes as "industrial, electronic, experimental and orchestral cinematic pieces".

The game's kit, Anti-Citizen, came out in March 2020.
