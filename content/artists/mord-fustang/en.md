Rauno Roosnurm, an Estonian DJ and producer from Tallinn who was born in Kose in 1991, makes electro house as Mord Fustang. In 2011 the label Plasmapool released singles of his such as "Lick the Rainbow", and in March 2012 the Beatport Music Awards named him Breakthrough Artist of the Year. The Forza Horizon soundtrack used his music. In 2015 his first studio album, 9999 in 1, appeared on Magic Trooper, a label of his own. Murmurs, an EP, was the first record he made for Mau5trap.

His kit, Diamonds, came out in September 2015.
