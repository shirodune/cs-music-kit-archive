Jonathan Young makes heavy metal as a singer, musician and producer; a 2021 Nerdist profile put his YouTube audience at nearly two million. He has made metal covers of Disney songs and English versions of anime openings, a collection of sea shanty metal, and, separately, songs inspired by Helldivers 2. He also writes original music. His work spans pirate, Viking and zombie metal, and he has made a version of All For Me Grog with Alestorm.

His kit, Starship Velociraptor, came out in August 2025 in the Deluge Music Kit Box.
