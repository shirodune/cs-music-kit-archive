The music of Halo started with Martin O'Donnell and Michael Salvatori. Together they scored each Halo game that Bungie made, from Combat Evolved in 2001 to Reach in 2010, and the Gregorian chant at the start of the main theme was O'Donnell's idea. The Halo 2 soundtrack was the first from a video game to break into the Billboard 200. Halo entered Classic FM's listener ranking at No. 244 in 2015.

The games 343 Industries went on to make brought new composers: Neil Davidge and Kazuma Jinnouchi wrote the music of Halo 4, and Jinnouchi scored Halo 5: Guardians alone. The Halo kit, The Master Chief Collection, came out in November 2019; owners of Halo: The Master Chief Collection on Steam received it once they had played the game long enough.
