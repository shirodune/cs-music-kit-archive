Wikipedia credits Mike Morasky as the composer of Counter-Strike: Global Offensive and of Counter-Strike 2, both developed by Valve. For Valve he also composed the music of Team Fortress 2, and his credits there include Portal 2 and Half-Life: Alyx. Outside games, the American composer, born in 1964, worked in visual effects on The Lord of the Rings and The Matrix film trilogies, and in 1986 he started the band Steel Pole Bath Tub with Dale Flattum.

Valve's two kits are CS:GO, the default music of Counter-Strike: Global Offensive, and Counter-Strike 2, the official music kit of Counter-Strike 2.
