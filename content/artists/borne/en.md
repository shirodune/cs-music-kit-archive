borne, from Montreal, makes bass music as a producer and DJ. He has been DJing since the age of 14; producing came later, as he was finishing high school. NIGHTMODE put out his EP Deep End at the end of 2023 and then Hush, a single he made with Alexis B.

His kit, Give It To Me, is one of the NIGHTMODE II Music Kits, which Valve released with NIGHTMODE Records in May 2026.
