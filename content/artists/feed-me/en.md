Feed Me is one of Jon Gooch's stage names; when he started producing he called himself Spor. Gooch, born in Hertfordshire, England, in 1984, works as a DJ and producer across electro house, dubstep and drum and bass. The Spell / Raw Chicken, a Feed Me release from 2008, came out on deadmau5's label mau5trap. For his Feed Me music he later set up his own label, Sotto Voce, which released his debut album Calamari Tuesday in 2013; it went to No. 3 on Billboard's Dance/Electronic Albums chart.

His kit, High Noon, was one of the nine kits that music kits launched with in October 2014.
