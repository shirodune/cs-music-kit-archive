Laura Shigihara was the lead composer and sound designer of Plants vs. Zombies. Her father is Japanese and her mother French American; she was born in the US and spent her childhood there and in Japan. Besides composing and writing songs, she makes games of her own: the adventure games Rakuen (2017) and Mr. Saitou (2023). She has also worked on To the Moon, World of Warcraft and Deltarune, and together with C418 she wrote the ending theme of the documentary Minecraft: The Story of Mojang.

Her kit, Work Hard, Play Hard, is in the Tacticians Music Kit Box of August 2021.
