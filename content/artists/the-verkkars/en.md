The Verkkars are Tomi Tamminen and Iiro Paakkari, two Finnish songwriters who also write for other artists. They started the duo half by accident in 2019, with a song made in one evening for their friends: EZ4ENCE, named after the chant of the Finnish team ENCE and full of references to its players and in-jokes.

The song went viral in Finland during ENCE's unexpected run to the final of the IEM Katowice Major in 2019, reaching No. 4 in Spotify's Finnish top 50, and the team walked on stage to it at the final. Valve got in touch after the tournament, and EZ4ENCE became a music kit that March. For Flashbang Dance (2021) they teamed up with the American Counter-Strike legend Jordan "n0thing" Gilbert, working with him remotely for nearly two years, and premiered the song at the final of the PGL Major Stockholm.
