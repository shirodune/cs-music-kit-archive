The Verkkars are Tomi Tamminen and Iiro Paakkari, two Finnish songwriters who also write for other artists. They started the duo half by accident in 2019, with a song made in one evening for their friends: EZ4ENCE, named after the chant of the Finnish team ENCE and full of references to its players and in-jokes.

In 2019 EZ4ENCE reached No. 4 on Spotify's Finnish top 50 while ENCE made its way to the final of the IEM Katowice Major, where the team walked on stage to it. Valve got in touch after the tournament, and the song became a music kit that March. Flashbang Dance (2021) was made with the American Counter-Strike player Jordan "n0thing" Gilbert, working remotely for nearly two years, and they gave it its first live performance at the final of the PGL Major Stockholm.
