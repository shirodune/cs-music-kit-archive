Sean Murray's scores include Call of Duty: World at War (2008) and Call of Duty: Black Ops (2010), the game True Crime: Streets of LA (2003) and the second season of Buffy the Vampire Slayer. The American composer, a son of the actor Don Murray, wrote his first film score, for the action film Scorpion (1986), while still a teenager. Santa Barbara, California, is where he grew up and attended the Brooks Institute film school.

His kit, A*D*8, was one of the nine kits that music kits launched with in October 2014.
