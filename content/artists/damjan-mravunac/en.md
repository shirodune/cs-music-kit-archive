Damjan Mravunac is a Croatian composer and sound designer. He has worked at the game studio Croteam since 1999 and became its audio director. His scores there include the Serious Sam games, The Talos Principle (2014) and The Talos Principle 2 (2023). He also helped develop a dynamic music system for PC games that moves between calm and combat music in real time, following how intense the play is. His own musical roots are in hard rock and heavy metal: he learned mostly on his own, playing in local bands and recording at home in Cubase.

His kit, The Talos Principle, came out in February 2015.
