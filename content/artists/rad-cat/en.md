Rad Cat is a DJ and producer from San Diego. As a first-generation Mexican-American he grew up with traditional Mexican music at home, and discovering deadmau5 in his first year of high school made him curious about making songs on a computer. He came up with the name at about 14, putting his favourite word next to his favourite animal. He works across genres, from pop-punk to disco, and his credits as a collaborator or remixer include Kevin Gates and Mike Posner.

His kit, Reason, came out in February 2024 in the NIGHTMODE Music Kit Box.
