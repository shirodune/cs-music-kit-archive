James and the Cold Gun are a rock band from Cardiff, Wales, with James Joseph on vocals and guitar and James Biss on lead guitar. Pearl Jam's Stone Gossard signed them to his label Loosegroove right after hearing them on KEXP. Their self-titled debut album came out in July 2023, followed in 2025 by Face In The Mirror, which the band produced themselves and Brendan O'Brien mixed. They have supported Pearl Jam and Guns N' Roses at Hyde Park in London.

Their kit, Chewing Glass, came out in August 2025 in the Deluge Music Kit Box.
