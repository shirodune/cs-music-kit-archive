Ghost is a rock band from Linköping, Sweden, that Tobias Forge started in 2006. The band performs in Satanic clerical costumes. Forge sings in character: first as Papa Emeritus I, II and III, then as Cardinal Copia, who became Papa Emeritus IV in 2020, and since 2025 as Papa V Perpetua. The other musicians appear as anonymous "Nameless Ghouls". Their song Cirice won Best Metal Performance at the 2016 Grammys, and their six studio albums run from Opus Eponymous (2010) to Skeletá (2025).

Their kit, Skeletá, came out in August 2025 in the Deluge Music Kit Box.
