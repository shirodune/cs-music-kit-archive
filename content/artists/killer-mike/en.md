Killer Mike, born Michael Santiago Render in 1975, is a rapper from Atlanta, Georgia. He made his first appearance on record on OutKast's album Stankonia (2000), and The Whole World, a song he recorded with OutKast, won the 2003 Grammy for Best Rap Performance by a Duo or Group. In 2013 he and the rapper and producer El-P became the duo Run the Jewels, whose four albums run from Run the Jewels (2013) to RTJ4 (2020). He has kept making solo records alongside the duo, and at the 66th Grammy Awards in 2024 he won three awards.

His kit, MICHAEL, came in the Deluge Music Kit Box in August 2025.
