Alex Costello sang in Roam, a pop punk band that started in Eastbourne, East Sussex, England, in September 2012 and announced its split on 21 June 2022. Hopeless Records signed them in 2014, after two self-released EPs, and released the EP Viewpoint (2015) and all three of their albums: Backbone (2016), Great Heights & Nosedives (2017) and Smile Wide (2019).

Their kit, Backbone, came in the StatTrak™ Radicals Box in November 2016.
