Sarah Schachner is an American composer for games, film and television who plays cello, violin, piano and synthesizer. She was born in Philadelphia in 1985 and grew up in its suburbs. The composer Brian Tyler, with whom she worked after studying at Berklee College of Music and moving to Los Angeles, first brought her in on Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 3. Games she has scored include Call of Duty: Infinite Warfare, Assassin's Creed Origins, Anthem and Call of Duty: Modern Warfare (2019), and the one for Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II earned her a Grammy nomination. For film, she scored Dan Trachtenberg's Prey (2022).

Her kit, KOLIBRI, is in the Tacticians Music Kit Box of August 2021.
