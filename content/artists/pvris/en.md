PVRIS is an American band from Lowell, Massachusetts, with Lynn Gunn as singer. The group goes back to 2009, when it was called Operation Guillotine; it became Paris in 2012 and, for legal reasons, PVRIS in 2013. Their first two albums came out on Rise Records: White Noise (2014) and All We Know of Heaven, All We Need of Hell (2017), which reached No. 4 in the UK. Use Me (2020) followed on Warner and Evergreen (2023) on Hopeless Records. At the 2015 Kerrang! Awards they won Best International Newcomer.

Their kit, Evergreen, came in the Deluge Music Kit Box in August 2025.
