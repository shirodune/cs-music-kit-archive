Hundredth comes from Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, and formed in 2008; singer and songwriter Chadwick Johnson fronts the band. Their early records were melodic hardcore, and their sound today is shoegaze, dream pop and alternative rock. Mediaskare Records put out When Will We Surrender (2010) and Let Go (2011); Hopeless Records released Free (2015) and RARE (2017); Somewhere Nowhere (2020) and Faded Splendor (2025) the band released itself.

Their kit, FREE, came in the StatTrak™ Radicals Box in November 2016.
