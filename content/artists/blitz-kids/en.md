Formed in 2006 under the name Rig Up Explosive, the band from Nantwich and Crewe in Cheshire, England, renamed itself Blitz Kids in 2009. Joe James sang, and the music was alternative rock and pop punk. Two EPs on Small Town Records (2010 and 2011) came first, then the debut album Vagrants & Vagabonds on Hassle Records in 2011 and the EP Never Die, also on Hassle, in 2012. Their second album, The Good Youth (2014), came out on Red Bull Records and peaked at No. 5 on the UK Rock & Metal Albums Chart. The band announced its split in July 2015.

Their kit, The Good Youth, came in the StatTrak™ Radicals Box in November 2016.
