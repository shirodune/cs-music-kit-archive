Dren McDonald is an American composer, songwriter and producer from Long Beach, California. In 2009 he founded Nerdtracks, which has since worked on more than 75 games. His own scores include Ravenwood Fair, Skulls of the Shogun and Gathering Sky, which won him three Game Audio Network Guild awards.

His first kit, Death's Head Demolition, credited simply to "Dren", was one of the nine that music kits launched with in October 2014; he describes it as over-the-top, superhero-inspired metal. Gunman Taco Truck (Masterminds, 2020) brings excerpts from his score for the indie game of the same name. For Coffee! Kofe! Kahveh! (Masterminds 2, 2024) he turned to Balkan brass street-party music, recorded with local horn players from bands such as Inspector Gadjé and Fanfare Zambaleta.
