Tigercub is a rock band from Brighton, England, with Jamie Hall on vocals and guitar, Jimi Wheelwright on bass and James Allix on drums. Hall and Allix, who had met as students in the city, began as a duo in 2011, and Wheelwright made it a trio a year later. Their albums run from Abstract Figures in the Dark (2016) through As Blue as Indigo (2021), No. 5 on the UK Rock & Metal chart, and The Perfume of Decay (2023) to Nets to Catch the Wind (2026). Along the way they have toured with Royal Blood and Blood Red Shoes, both in 2014, and played the Reading and Leeds Festivals in 2017.

Their kit, The Perfume of Decay, came in the Deluge Music Kit Box in August 2025.
