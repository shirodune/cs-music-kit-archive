Meechy Darko grew up in a Jamaican family in Flatbush, Brooklyn, on the same block as Erick Arc Elliott. Since 2010 the two have rapped with Zombie Juice in the trio Flatbush Zombies, whose 2016 album 3001: A Laced Odyssey debuted at No. 10 on the Billboard 200. Born Demetri Simms in 1989, he is also part of the collective Beast Coast. Loma Vista Recordings has released his solo albums, Gothic Luxury (2022) and Doses (2023).

His kit, Gothic Luxury, came in the Initiators Music Kit Box in August 2022.
