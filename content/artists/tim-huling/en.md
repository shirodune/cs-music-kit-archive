Tim Huling writes and orchestrates music for the screen, for games and for the concert stage, and teaches screen scoring as a professor at Berklee College of Music, where he studied. He joined the teaching staff of its film scoring department in 2014. On screen he wrote the music for the documentary Inside Passage and orchestrated the film Mad Money and the game Planetary Annihilation; his concert works include the ballet Demeter & Persephone and Astrobiology, for live orchestra with film. In 2017 he received the Ernst Bacon Memorial Award in the Performance of American Music.

His kits are Neo Noir, in the Masterminds Music Kit Box of April 2020, and Devil's Paintbrush, in the Masterminds 2 Music Kit Box of August 2024.
