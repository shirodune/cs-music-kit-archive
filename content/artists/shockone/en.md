ShockOne is Karl Thomas, an Australian producer and DJ born in Esperance and living in Perth, whose music covers drum and bass, dubstep, drumstep and electro house. Two of his school bandmates, Rob Swire and Gareth McGrillen, went on to form Pendulum: Thomas had played drums with them in a metal band called Xygen, which stopped once Pendulum got going in the early 2000s. ShockOne was the electronic project he started next. He has released three albums: Universus (2013, Viper Recordings), A Dark Machine (2019) and Organism Algorithm (2024, on his own Dark Machine Records). His single Follow Me won Best Independent Dance, Electronica or Club Single at the 2021 AIR Awards.

His kit, Voices, is one of the NIGHTMODE II Music Kits, which Valve released with NIGHTMODE Records in May 2026.
