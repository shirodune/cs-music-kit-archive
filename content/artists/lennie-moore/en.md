Lennie Moore's game credits include the Outcast series, Star Wars: The Old Republic, whose soundtrack album won a G.A.N.G. Award, the anniversary editions of Halo: Combat Evolved and Halo 2, and Red Orchestra 2: Rising Storm. Besides games he has written, arranged and orchestrated for film, television and advertising. He took a Bachelor of Music in jazz composition at Berklee College of Music, then studied orchestral conducting in Los Angeles for six years. Today he teaches at the San Francisco Conservatory of Music.

His kit, Java Havana Funkaloo, came out in September 2015; he also made a music pack for Dota 2's The International 2016.
