DRYDEN is a project of Dryden Brown, a producer based in Las Vegas who used to release music under the name Lemay. Brown has said that when he began it, he asked himself "what would Dryden listen to?" Two bootleg packs on SoundCloud, MINUTES PAST Vol. 1 and Vol. 2, gave listeners an early preview of the new project's direction.

His kit, Feel The Power, came out in February 2024 in the NIGHTMODE Music Kit Box.
