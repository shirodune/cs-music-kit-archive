Daniel Sadowski is a composer and producer who lives in Olympia, Washington. He has worked in the games industry since around 2005 and has composed music for more than 100 projects, among them Dota 2, A Boy and His Blob, Megamind and Man of Steel. He also votes on the Grammys as a member of the Recording Academy.

He has been part of music kits from the first day: Crimson Assault was one of the nine kits they launched with in October 2014. Total Domination and The 8-Bit Kit followed in 2015, Eye of the Dragon in the Masterminds Music Kit Box in 2020 and Dead Shot in the Masterminds 2 Music Kit Box in 2024. In December 2021 he released the music of Total Domination as a soundtrack album.
