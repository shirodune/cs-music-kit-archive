Scarlxrd is the stage name of Marius Listhrop, an English rapper born in Wolverhampton in 1994 and seen as a pioneer of trap metal, a mix of trap and heavy metal. He started out as the singer of the nu metal band Myth City and as the YouTuber Mazzi Maz, and released his first song as Scarlxrd in 2016. The video for "Heart Attack" passed 100 million views on YouTube. His album Dxxm came out on Island Records in 2018; he later moved to his own label, Lxrd Records.

Both of his kits are named after his songs: King, Scar came in December 2019, and CHAIN$AW.LXADXUT., after "Chain$aw", in March 2021.
