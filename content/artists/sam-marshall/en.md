Sam Marshall is a composer for games in the San Francisco Bay Area. He joined the music department of Sony Interactive Entertainment in 2013 and scored Concrete Genie (2019) for PlayStation, giving its themes to flute, cello and an analogue synthesiser. He has also worked on the music of Call of Duty games, from WWII to Modern Warfare III.

Bodacious, in the Masterminds Music Kit Box in 2020, was his first Counter-Strike kit; Clutch followed in Masterminds 2 in 2024.
