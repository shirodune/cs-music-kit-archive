Moments CSGO takes the Moments in its title from Darude's fourth studio album, which Warner Music Finland released on 14 August 2015 as his first full-length release in eight years. PCGamesN reported that Darude had announced on Reddit that he was working with Valve on a pack called Moments, timed to go with the album. The kit followed on 24 September 2015.
