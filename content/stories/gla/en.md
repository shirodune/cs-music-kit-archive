Twin Atlantic's fourth album, GLA, came out through Red Bull Records on 9 September 2016, less than three months before the kit. The title is the airport code of the band's home city, Glasgow. Jacknife Lee produced the album in Los Angeles, and it reached No. 9 in the UK, the band's second top-ten album there.
