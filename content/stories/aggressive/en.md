Beartooth's second album, Aggressive, came out on 3 June 2016 on Red Bull Records and UNFD. Caleb Shomo produced it and performed the vocals and all instruments, and David Bendeth mixed it. The album topped the US Top Alternative Albums chart, and in December 2020 the band reissued it with a new mix and master by Shomo.
