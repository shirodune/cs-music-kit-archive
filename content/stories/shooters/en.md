The song behind the kit came out on SoundCloud in April 2017. In an interview published alongside the release, Juelz told Daily Hive it was one of his favourite tracks so far, "a little more left field" than his other work, with plenty of synths and Auto-Tuned vocals.

Those vocals are his own and his friend Landyn's. In the studio the two kept singing one line through Auto-Tune, "I got shooters dressed in camo", which Juelz had picked up from Quavo on "Oh My Dis Side", a track with Travis Scott; the song grew out of those takes.
