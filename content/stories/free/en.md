FREE shares its name with Hundredth's third album, Free, released in June 2015 as their first on Hopeless Records. On RARE (2017), the album after it, the band took up a sound drawing on shoegaze and indie rock; Chadwick Johnson said: "We drained our 'hardcore' vein on Free."
