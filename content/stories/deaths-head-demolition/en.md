Nerdtracks, the company Dren founded, handled the composition, production, mixing and mastering of Death's Head Demolition, as well as its dynamic interactive music design, built "according to detailed specs provided by Valve Software". Cody Miles drew the artwork. Nils Frykdahl, a member of Sleepytime Gorilla Museum and Faun Fables, narrated the trailer. According to Nerdtracks, Valve's video about reintroducing the Train map also uses this music.
