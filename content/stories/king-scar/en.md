The song King, Scar is on Chaxsthexry, the album Scarlxrd released on 1 April 2017; "Chain$aw" and "Heart Attack" are on it too. He put out the "King, Scar" video a week after the one for "Chain$aw" (31 May 2017). In his view, the "Heart Attack" video, which followed in June, caught on so quickly because he had kept putting out videos, these two in particular.
