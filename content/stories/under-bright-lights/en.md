The kit shares its name with Under Bright Lights, a drum and bass single TWERL and Ekko & Sidetrack released on NIGHTMODE in March 2022, sung by Indy Skies from Auckland. Both acts tied the song to the rave. TWERL said they wanted it to let people "come alive under the bright lights of the rave"; Ekko & Sidetrack called it a tune "about letting go and being able to set yourself free".
