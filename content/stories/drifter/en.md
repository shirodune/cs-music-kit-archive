On his site, Levine calls the music "a gritty homage to the Spaghetti Western". On 19 July 2023 Matt Levine released an EP titled Drifter (Original Video Game Soundtrack) on his company Uncle Vector's Audio Lab, with four tracks: Grit, Breaking In, Tick Tock BOOM! and MVP Anthem. Less than a month later the first three were also on the album MASTERMINDS MUSIC KIT BOX (Original Video Game Soundtrack).
