The voice on Bachram is Peter Hollens, an American singer and producer whose a cappella work goes back to 1999, when he co-founded a group at the University of Oregon; he was also one of the soloists on Wintory's score for The Banner Saga. In June 2020 Wintory put the kit's menu track, Bachram, on Bandcamp, with the menu music of Desert Fire as a bonus. In 2023 it was the first track of the album MASTERMINDS MUSIC KIT BOX (Original Video Game Soundtrack), followed by two more Wintory pieces, The Count and Savage Highlands.
