Killer Mike's sixth studio album, Michael, was released by VLNS and Loma Vista Recordings on 16 June 2023. It was his first solo studio album in eleven years, and he described it as his "come home moment musically". André 3000, Future and CeeLo Green are among the guests; No I.D., James Blake and El-P are among the producers. At the 66th Grammy Awards the album won Best Rap Album, and its song Scientists & Engineers won both Best Rap Performance and Best Rap Song.
