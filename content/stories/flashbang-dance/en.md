The track is the official song for n0thing's trademark Flashbang Dance, which Win.gg links to Cloud9's 2015 roster: asked about the dance in a fan Q&A at a HyperX-sponsored boot camp, Tyler "Skadoodle" Latham put on some music, and he and n0thing showed the moves. Its moves mimic a player who is blinded by a flashbang and then recovers to take aim.

n0thing also raps early in the song, with Counter-Strike rhymes such as "even if I go blind I can still see all my haters". The animated music video shows the group cruising around Long A on Dust II.
