The kit shares its name with Feel The Power, which Dancing Astronaut described as DRYDEN's first original release on NIGHTMODE. NIGHTMODE, a label under Insomniac Music Group, released it on 12 May 2023 as catalogue number NM078. The label's press text calls it "an electro-charged cut employing surging saw synth riffs", and Dancing Astronaut heard Knife Party and Justice in it.
