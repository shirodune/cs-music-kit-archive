All Distortions Are Intentional, the album named in the kit's description, is Neck Deep's fourth studio album, released by Hopeless Records on 24 July 2020 and produced by Matt Squire. It is a concept album set in Sonderland, a name that blends "wonderland" and "sonder", and its lead characters are Jett, a loner there, and Alice.
