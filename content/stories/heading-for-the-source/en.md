Heading for the Source is a selection of tracks rather than a single song. 3kliksphilip described it as "comprising of tracks made by my Dad and myself", and the Steam Workshop submission credits 3kliksphilip and Steventhedreamer.

The kit was reworked in public before Valve added it in August 2022; Win.gg traced its first version back to March 2021. 3kliksphilip presented it in a video called "I have made a Music Kit", then revised several tracks after feedback and invited viewers to vote on further changes. The updated version, he wrote, took in "all of the changes and suggestions" and was uploaded "to the right bit of the workshop for music kits".
