Matt Levine's three tracks on the album MASTERMINDS II Music Kit Box (Original Video Game Soundtrack), which came out the same day as the box, are Agency, Ready or Not and Now or Forever. He produced the whole album with help from the San Francisco Conservatory of Music, where he teaches: its students and faculty took part, and roughly half the music was mixed in Dolby Atmos in its studios. On two of Levine's own tracks the bass is played by Kurt Ribak, a jazz musician from the Bay Area. "This was a dream project for me to do at SFCM," Levine said.
