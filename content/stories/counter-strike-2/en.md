The kit shares its name with Counter-Strike 2, a three-track album released under the name Valve Studio Orchestra on 1 November 2023; Steam lists it as "The official soundtrack of Counter-Strike 2", and its first track is also called Counter-Strike 2. Wikipedia names Ipecac Recordings as the label behind its digital release. On Bandcamp, Valve Studio Orchestra is also the name on the Team Fortress 2 and Dota 2 soundtracks.
