Desert Fire is one of the nine kits that music kits started with on 10 October 2014. Valve announced them as "new music exclusively made for CS:GO by various artists and composers", and its own description of this one promises a "deadly, admittedly insane, tango".

The tango is played by real musicians: Tom Strahle on guitar, Taylor Davis on violin and Amy Tatum on flute. Six years later, in June 2020, Austin Wintory put the main menu music on Bandcamp as a bonus track to his second kit, Bachram, calling it "the original 2014 CSGO Music Kit I did".
