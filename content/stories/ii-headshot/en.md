For his second kit Jocke Skog brought in Per Nilsson of Scar Symmetry, who plays lead guitar on it; the Bandcamp release credits the main menu track to both of them. Apart from that the credits name only Skog, who also programmed, mixed and mastered it; the Bandcamp version adds an extended take of the bomb-planted music and two more main menu pieces.
