According to Valve's description, the kit's music comes from Hotline Miami, the 2012 top-down shooter that Jonatan Söderström and Dennis Wedin made as Dennaton Games, with Devolver Digital as publisher. The placeholder music they first used could not be licensed, so they turned to free downloads on Bandcamp; Söderström said they went through as many as two thousand tracks, and M.O.O.N. was one of the finds. Scattle came to them instead, after reading about the game on their development blog, and wrote new music for it. The finished soundtrack runs to 22 tracks.
