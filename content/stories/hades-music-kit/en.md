Valve awarded the kit to owners of Hades on Steam once they had played enough of it, more than five hours according to PCGamesN. Supergiant Games opened Hades to players in early access in December 2018 and released it in full on 17 September 2020.

Korb has called the game's score "Mediterranean prog rock Halloween". The music sets a Greek bouzouki, a Turkish lavta and a bağlama against metal guitar, and Ashley Barrett's voice runs through the soundtrack.
