The Chinese CS team made Ay Hey together with the ethnic music and dance composition centre of Guangxi Arts University, where Professor Dai Wei and the teacher Zhao Wan composed and arranged it. The male voices draw on Zhuang liaoge songs and the female voices on Zhuang three-part folk singing, two traditions listed as national intangible cultural heritage in 2008. They are sung by Lu Shunhong and Yu Xianzhuan, recognised bearers of the liaoge tradition at national and regional level, and by the young folk-singing group Shaoli. The arrangement adds the bolie, a Zhuang wind instrument that looks like a suona but sounds softer.

The title is an interjection taken from the song, the sound of a player enjoying a win or teasing a friend. The team called Ay Hey the second music kit from China and the first in a Chinese minority language.
