Gunman Taco Truck is a post-apocalyptic roguelike that Romero Games released in January 2017: after an accidental nuclear strike, you drive a taco truck towards Winnipeg. Donovan Brathwaite-Romero came up with the idea in 2014, and his parents, John and Brenda Romero, helped him make it.

Dren had worked with John and Brenda Romero on many projects and told John he "HAD to write the music for that." In a 2015 interview he called the score "apocalyptic mariachi music", written for two trumpets, two violins, baritone sax, accordion, drums and percussion, guitar and bass. He played the guitar and bass himself and had live players for the rest. Many parts went through mechanical filters, old air ducts among them, or unusual microphones on the way to the recording.
