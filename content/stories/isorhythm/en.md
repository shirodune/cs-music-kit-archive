IsoRhythm shares its name with the label Matt Lange started in 2011 so that he could put out his own music on his own timetable. "Isorhythm was simply an outlet to release whatever I wanted, whenever I wanted," he told The Fearless Nomad. His albums Isolated (2020) and Dichotomy (2022) came out on it.
