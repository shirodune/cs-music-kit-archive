CHAIN$AW.LXADXUT. takes the CHAIN$AW part of its title from "Chain$aw", a song on Scarlxrd's album Chaxsthexry (1 April 2017), the same record as "King, Scar". The music video for "Chain$aw" came out on 31 May 2017.
