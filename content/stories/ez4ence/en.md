The title is a chant from Twitch chat: it's always an easy game for ENCE. Win.gg traced the lyrics to the Finnish scene's running jokes, among them ENCE general manager Joona "Natu" Leppänen's hot tub, Sami "xseveN" Laasanen managing only "no words" in an interview, the 1982 Formula 1 champion Keke Rosberg and Finland's ice hockey world championship of 1995.

The Verkkars first posted the song on YouTube, and a record label joined only later. According to Yle, Valve turned it into a kit because the Counter-Strike community had asked for it so strongly. By November 2021, Warner Music Finland reported more than 11 million Spotify streams, enough for double platinum, and over 13 million YouTube views for the video.
