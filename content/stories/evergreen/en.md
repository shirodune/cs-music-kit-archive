PVRIS's fourth studio album, Evergreen, was released by Hopeless Records on 14 July 2023; its title song came out as a single in June 2023. Lynn Gunn called the record "a reclamation of control in our post-pandemic culture". Mike Shinoda is one of its producers. The album reached No. 25 on the UK Albums Chart and No. 1 on the UK Rock & Metal Albums Chart.
