Tigercub's third album, The Perfume of Decay, was released on 2 June 2023 by Loosegroove Records, a label co-founded by Pearl Jam guitarist Stone Gossard. Jamie Hall wrote and produced it himself. Hall picked up the title phrase from a film; he said it "seemed to sum up the bittersweet nature of growing up". The title song reached No. 28 on the US Mainstream Rock chart.
