On 12 August 2021, the day the box came out, Shigihara also released a two-minute single, Work Hard, Play Hard (CS:GO Music Kit). When she announced the kit on her blog the next day, she summed it up as "Disco, 90s dance, retro video games, synth wave, and just a hint of Plants vs. Zombies".
