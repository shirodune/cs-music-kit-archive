Ghost's sixth studio album, Skeletá, was released by Loma Vista Recordings on 25 April 2025. Tobias Forge produced it under the alias Gene Walker, and the band presented a new figurehead, Papa V Perpetua, when they announced it. It entered the US Billboard 200 at No. 1, a first for the band.
