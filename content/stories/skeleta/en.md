The kit shares its name with Skeletá, Ghost's sixth studio album, which Loma Vista Recordings released on 25 April 2025. Tobias Forge produced it under the alias Gene Walker, and the band presented a new figurehead, Papa V Perpetua, when they announced it. It entered the US Billboard 200 at No. 1, a first for the band.
