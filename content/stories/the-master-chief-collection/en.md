The kit was part of a cross-promotion between Valve and 343 Industries, which also put a Halo sticker capsule in the in-game store. Valve awarded it to owners of Halo: The Master Chief Collection on Steam who had played the game for five hours or more. The collection first came out on Xbox One in November 2014 and reached PC one game at a time, starting with Halo: Reach on 3 December 2019.

The kit uses nine Halo pieces, among them Ghosts of Reach on the main menu, The Last Spartan for a won round and Halo Theme MJOLNIR Mix as the MVP anthem.
