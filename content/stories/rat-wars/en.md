HEALTH's fifth studio album, Rat Wars, was produced by Stint and released by Loma Vista Recordings on 7 December 2023; it reached No. 4 on the UK Rock & Metal Albums chart. Its single Hateful was first heard in an April 2023 update of the game Ultrakill, and to promote the album the band put out Rat Wars Survivors, a game in the style of Vampire Survivors.
