On 4 October 2024 Dren McDonald put the kit's music on Bandcamp as an EP, Coffee! Kofé! Kahveh! (from Counterstrike 2). Besides the three pieces released on the Masterminds II soundtrack, it adds Dirty Chai MVP, a reworked version of the MVP music. The credited players are Gregory Jenkins on alto and clarinet, Max ML on trumpet, Paul Marini on baritone horn and Eric Darken on percussion, with McDonald himself on guitars and bass.
