The Good Youth shares its name with Blitz Kids' second and last album, which they recorded in Los Angeles in summer 2013 with producer John Feldmann and released through Red Bull Records in January 2014. By the time the kit came out in November 2016, the band no longer existed: they had played their farewell shows in October 2015.
