ISOxo released the house track inhuman on NIGHTMODE in October 2022. It had already been played nearly two years earlier, about halfway through his KIDSGONEMAD set on RL Grime's Halloween IX livestream in October 2020; Dancing Astronaut called it a "haunting house track".
