Austin Wintory's starting point, as he put it, was a "little weird experiment": an Appalachian hoedown pushed into club music, with some Eastern European colour and operatic singing on top. Paul Cartwright plays the fiddles, Tom Strahle the guitars and banjos and Eric Snoza the upright bass, Laura Intravia sings, and Joris Hoogsteder added synth production. Wintory released the music on Bandcamp as three tracks on 20 August 2024, the day the box came out, thanking "Matt Levine and the rest of the Masterminds team".
