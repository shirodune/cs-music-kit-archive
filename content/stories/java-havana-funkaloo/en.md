When the kit came out, Lennie Moore wrote on his site that this time he "had to follow a whim that Counter Strike was just not funky enough". In the notes to the music he recalls asking what kind of music the existing kits lacked for the game's fans, and answering: "It's missing the funk!"

The Bandcamp release credits eight musicians. Moore plays electric bass, with Walter Simonsen on trumpet, Erm Navarro on trombone, Andy Suzuki on alto and tenor saxophone, Phil Moore on baritone saxophone, Nick Manson on keyboards, Ken Lasaine on electric guitar and Mark Yeend on drums. In June 2023, after "many years of requests from fans", he released the music on Bandcamp as an album of fourteen tracks.
