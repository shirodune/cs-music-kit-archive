In Austin Wintory's words: "I decided to see what would happen if I smashed together beer garden-type polka and full on metal!" Ben Rosenblum plays the accordion, Tom Strahle the guitars, Christian Pacaud the electric bass and Eric Snoza the upright bass, with additional synths by Joris Hoogsteder; Wintory produced and mixed it. He put the track on Bandcamp on 12 August 2021, the day the Tacticians Music Kit Box came out, about three weeks after releasing a single called Mocha Petal on 23 July.
