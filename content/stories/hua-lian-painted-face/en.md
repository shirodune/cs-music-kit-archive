The Chinese CS team presented 花脸 Hua Lian (Painted Face) as the first Chinese-language music kit in the game. The poet and scholar Xue Baoqin wrote the lyrics, and the music is by Luo Xinchang, art director of the youth troupe at the Shaanxi Provincial Opera Research Institute, and the composer Zhu Hongliang. Zhao Yangwu, a winner of China's Plum Blossom Award for theatre, sings the lead and Yang Jing the female Qinqiang part; Che Hu arranged and mixed the song and plays guitar.

In Qinqiang, hualian is the everyday name for the jing role. The lyrics, sung in the Guanzhong dialect of Shaanxi and in Mandarin, work in the Chinese names of in-game finishes, among them 咆哮, 巨龙, 雷击 and 野火. According to the team, the song later appeared in a special programme of CCTV's Spring Festival Gala.
