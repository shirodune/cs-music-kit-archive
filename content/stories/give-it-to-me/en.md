Give It To Me shares its name with a borne single that NIGHTMODE released in August 2024, his fourth record with the label. The release description has a female vocal hook over a dubstep production, and Peekaboo, Hamdi, Ternion Sound and Eliminate were playing it early on. borne said: "I'm eager to release this track because of its deep and dark vibe."
