On And On shares its name with a single by Repiet and Julia Kleijn that NIGHTMODE released on 29 August 2025; for Repiet it followed his 2023 NIGHTMODE debut, Falling For You. The track itself draws on Counter-Strike. "I wanted to capture that epic, high-stakes energy I grew up loving in 'Counter-Strike,' and turn it into something cinematic and powerful for the dancefloor," Repiet said, and the release notes pitched it as "the soundtrack to a final-round clutch".
