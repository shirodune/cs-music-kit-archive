The Talos Principle is a narrative puzzle game by Croteam, published by Devolver Digital in December 2014 and written by Tom Jubert and Jonas Kyratzes. For its score, Mravunac's site says, he "deliberately abandoned driving rhythms and memorable melodies in favour of ambient, layered soundscapes". In December 2015 he put the music of the game on Bandcamp as an album of 28 tracks.
