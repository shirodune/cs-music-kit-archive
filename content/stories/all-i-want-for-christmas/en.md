The kit's title matches the opening words of a Christmas song Valve released for the band in December 2009: All I Want for Christmas (Is to Kick Your Ass). Shacknews reported that, in the band's fiction, the song was a forgotten recording from 1998, made for a Christmas album the Riders had "grudgingly agreed" to join.
