On Jocke Skog's Bandcamp page for his kits, Metal comes with a short note: "The first release. Inspired by the fact that Valve had asked Slayer before me. A classic thrash metal sound." The credits name Skog alone, for the music as well as its programming, mix and master. The Bandcamp release adds two bonus tracks, two more main menu pieces and a "first chaotic version" of the bomb-planted music.
